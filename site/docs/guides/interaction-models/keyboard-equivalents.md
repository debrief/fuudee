# Keyboard equivalents

How every map action can be done without a mouse.

*The model, its sequence diagram and its reference implementation are written in Phase 3. Version 0 gathers the requirements it must satisfy.*

## Requirements cited

| SRD | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| NFR-13 | The interface is operable by keyboard; every map action (dragging a track, drawing an annotation, measuring) has a keyboard or form-based equivalent; themes, including high contrast, are honoured. |

## Model

*Written so far for annotations and for moving, rotating, shearing and stretching tracks; the rest of the map follows in Phase 3.*

| Map action | Without a pointer |
| --- | --- |
| Draw a line, area, circle, ellipse, label or marker | `Tab` to the map's *Draw an annotation* toolbar, `Enter` on a kind; the arrow keys pan the map under the cross in its middle, `Space` places a point there, `Enter` finishes, `Backspace` removes the last, `Escape` cancels. Or **Add annotation…** and type the positions ([annotations](annotations.md), items 1 to 6). |
| Drag a corner, centre, radius, axis or label | `Tab` to its handle; the arrow keys move it 5 pixels (25 with `Shift`); the run of presses is one step when they stop, on `Enter`, or when the keyboard moves on (presses made while it is saved follow as the next step); `Escape` puts it back. Or type the change on its properties form. |
| Add a corner between two | Arrow keys on the handle between them. |
| Remove a corner | `Delete` or `Backspace` on its handle. |
| Drag a track, or its fixes in the time window | `Tab` to the selected track's move handle; the arrow keys move it 5 pixels (25 with `Shift`), and the run of presses is one step, as for an annotation's handle. *Whole track* and *Time window* are buttons on the map. Or **Move track…** and type the distance and bearing ([track operations](track-operations.md), item 11). |
| Rotate a track | `Tab` to its rotate handle; `Right` and `Up` turn it 1° clockwise, `Left` and `Down` anticlockwise, 10° with `Shift`; one step for the run. The pivot handle moves with the arrow keys. Or **Rotate track…**, with the pivot and angle typed. |
| Shear or stretch a track | `Tab` to a diamond beside the first or last fix. Shearing, the arrow keys move it 5 pixels (25 with `Shift`), the other end held; stretching (*Stretch* on the map), `Up` and `Right` make it 0.1 kn faster, `Down` and `Left` slower, 1 kn with `Shift`. One step for the run. Or **Shear track…** (course and speed made good) and **Stretch track…** (speed) ([track operations](track-operations.md#shearing-and-stretching)). |
| Split or merge tracks | **Split track at current time…** and **Merge selected tracks** on the outline's context menu (**Split…** and **Merge** under *Track*, below the outline, in the playground); there is no map gesture for either. |
| Right-click a fix (or press and hold it on a touch screen) to colour, label or comment on it | **Fixes: colour, label or comment…** on the outline's context menu: the fix list, a list to type in (a time finds a fix), then the action. In the playground, **Fixes…** under *Track*: `Up` and `Down` move along the list, `Enter` or `Space` chooses ([track operations](track-operations.md#cleaning-tracks), item 3). |
| Move a single fix | Zoomed in far enough that its fixes have rings, `Tab` to a fix's ring; the arrow keys move it 5 pixels (25 with `Shift`), one step for the run. Or **Move a fix…** and type the position ([track operations](track-operations.md#single-fixes-and-dead-reckoning), items 5 and 6). |
| Set how often tracks are labelled, marked with a symbol and given a direction arrow; a fix always showing its label or symbol | Select the tracks in the outline (`Enter`, `Ctrl+Shift+Enter` to add), then **Frequencies: labels, symbols and arrows…** on the outline's menu or the Command Palette: three lists, one after the other, an interval typed in any (**Frequencies…** under *Track* in the playground: a form of three choosers, `Tab` between them). A fix's own: **Fixes: colour, label or comment…**, then **Always show its time label** or **Always show its symbol** (**Time label** and **Symbol** in the playground's fix list) ([labels, symbols and arrows](labels-symbols-arrows.md)). There is no map gesture. |
| Plot a track over the ground or by dead reckoning | Choosing as it is imported, from a list; afterwards **Plot over the ground or by dead reckoning…** on the outline's menu (**OTG/DR…** in the playground); there is no map gesture. |
| Check jumps on the map, or a smoothing preview | **Find and remove jumps…** lists each jump, ticked, in a list read by screen readers; **Smooth track…** says how many fixes would move and the most any would, in words, before Apply ([track operations](track-operations.md#cleaning-tracks), items 1 and 2). |
| Choose the Tote's tracks | **Primary** and **Add** in the Tote are lists; `Delete` on a secondary removes it. A track's menu in the outline (`Shift+F10` or the menu key; press and hold on a touch screen) has **Set as Tote primary**, **Add to Tote** and **Remove from Tote** ([Tote](tote.md#keyboard-and-touch)). There is no map gesture. |
| Choose a narrative entry, a note or a period to move current time to it; read along while playing | The Timeline is a list: `Up`, `Down`, `Page Up`, `Page Down`, `Home` and `End` move through it, `Enter` or `Space` moves current time to the entry, `F2` changes a narrative entry, `Delete` deletes it, `N` adds one, `/` searches; `Ctrl+Z` undoes the plot's last step from the Timeline except in its text boxes (the search box and the form), where it undoes the typing ([Timeline and narrative](timeline.md#keyboard-and-touch)). The entry on the map beside its track needs no gesture. |
| Click one of an ambiguous cut's two lines to keep it; right-click (or press and hold) a cut for its menu; read a cut's bearings by pointing at it | A sensor's menu in the Outline (`Shift+F10` or the menu key; press and hold on a touch screen): **Ambiguous bearings: keep port or starboard…**, then **Keep port bearing**, **Keep starboard bearing** or **Make ambiguous again**, then **The cut at the current time**, **Cuts in the time window**, **Selected cuts** or **All cuts**, each saying how many cuts it holds; one step. **Cut at current time…** on the same menu says the bearings and sides of the cut the map emphasises and opens its menu (keep either bearing, ambiguous again, select it): move current time to reach any cut. The Command Palette has both. In the playground, **Port…**, **Starboard…** and **Both again…** under *Sensor* below the Outline, and a sensor row's menu. A sensor's details in the Outline say how many cuts are ambiguous and resolved ([sensors and bearings](sensors.md)). |
| Choose which catalogue item supplies a period of a track; take new data; confirm or refuse a platform | Coverage: `Tab` to a period's list, pick with `↑` `↓` (no step yet) and press `Enter` or **Set**, the keyboard's way for clicking a source's bar; **Take**, **Not now**, **Same platform**, **Not this platform**, **Unlink** and **Reviewed** are buttons. In VS Code's Catalogues, an item's or a track's menu (`Shift+F10` or the menu key) has the same. There is no map gesture ([Collation](collation.md#keyboard-and-touch)). |
| Export the map as an image; find the plot an image came from; leave an earlier revision shown read-only | **Export image…** (the Command Palette, the map's title bar, the Outline's menu): a list of sizes, two tick boxes (`Space`), then the save dialog. **Find source of image…**: a list, then the open dialog or the ID typed and `Enter`. On the map, `Tab` to **Go to latest** and `Enter`. In the playground, the *Export image* panel's lists, tick box and buttons, `Enter` in its ID box. There is no map gesture ([Exported images](export.md#keyboard-and-touch)). |
| Follow a walkthrough | VS Code's Get Started page, its own keyboard; in the playground, the *Walkthroughs* panel: a list to choose one, each step a button that opens it, its button the step's own. Every step says its keyboard equivalent ([Walkthroughs](walkthroughs.md)). |
| Rearrange the browser's panels: drag a tab to another group, or to a group's edge to split it | `Tab` moves through the layout group by group, each group's tab then what its panel holds; `←` `→` along a tab strip, `Enter` or `Space` to bring a panel to the front; `Shift+F10`, the menu key or the group's **⋯** opens the panel's menu: **Move to left: Outline, Catalogue** for each other group, **Move to a group of its own**, **Reset layout** ([browser layout](browser-layout.md)). The top bar's **Import…**, **Export** and **Try** open with `Enter`, and `Escape` closes them. |
| Clear the selection | `Escape` on the map; or the outline, a native tree. |
| Pan, zoom | Arrow keys, and `+` and `-`, with the map focused. |

## Reference implementation

`vscode/webview/mapedit.ts` (keys while drawing and on handles) and `vscode/webview/drawing.ts`; `vscode/webview/trackmap.ts` and `vscode/webview/trackedit.ts` for a track's handles. The playground tests `vscode/test/play/drawing.mjs` and `vscode/test/play/tracks.mjs` place a marker, change an area, move and rotate a track, `vscode/test/play/shear.mjs` shears and stretches one, and `vscode/test/play/fixdrag.mjs` moves a single fix, from the keyboard alone; `vscode/test/play/tote.mjs` chooses the Tote's tracks from the keyboard and by press and hold; `vscode/test/play/narrative.mjs` reads, chooses, adds, changes and deletes narrative entries from the keyboard; `vscode/test/play/sensors.mjs` keeps a side of a sensor's cuts in the window from the keyboard.
